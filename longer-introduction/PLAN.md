## Decisions to know about

- The intro never says what happened on 14 May or after. The name "Israel" appears only as one of five candidate names under blank 4, as in the game's own handout.
- The page says the cabinet "decided to go ahead" on 12 May. Kramer shows there was no recorded vote on the American truce, only general agreement; the page makes no vote-count claim.
- The 1956 UN map is used for the partition plan. It prints "Map No. 103.1(b), February 1956," and the caption says so.
